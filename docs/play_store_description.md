## Full Description
**Welcome to Cozy Doubling – Focus together, unhurried and calm.**

Do you find it easier to start and finish tasks when someone else is working alongside you? Welcome to the gentle power of **body doubling**. Cozy Doubling is a calm focus app built for anyone who wants to stay productive without the stress, noise, or pressure of traditional hustle culture.

**The Shared Cozy Room**
Instead of competitive leaderboards, harsh streak-shame, or loud alarms, Cozy Doubling centers on one peaceful shared space: **The Cozy Room**. Set your tasks, join alongside fellow doublers, and feel the quiet accountability of a library or shared studio—motivating yet deeply restful.

**Key Features:**
🌿 **The Shared Cozy Room:** Add your session tasks, mark what you are actively working on, and see real-time progress from others focusing alongside you.
📱 **Stay Focused Across Apps:** Keep your session alive cleanly in the background with an ongoing notification (complete tasks or finish right from the notification shade) and an optional floating Picture-in-Picture mini-widget while you study or work in other apps.
🌱 **Your Growing Oasis & Leaves:** Earn Leaves for your verified focus time and completed tasks, track your Journey, and unlock cozy Material color themes crafted to make your workspace feel like yours.
🚫 **100% Ad-Free & Distraction-Free:** No ad banners, no pop-up check-ins interrupting your flow, and no data selling—just a calm, focused space for your best work.
🤝 **Friends & Connect Codes:** Connect privately with study buddies using unique 8-character Connect Codes, see friends' recent focus stories, and get notified when friends enter the Cozy Room.
🔒 **Safety & Control First:** Block users immediately (in rooms, friends lists, and notifications), manage blocked users anytime in Settings, report inappropriate behavior, and delete your account permanently in one tap whenever you wish.
✨ **Supporter Themes:** Unlock exclusive Supporter Pass themes while helping keep Cozy Doubling independent, calm, and ad-free.

**Who is Cozy Doubling for?**
• Students looking for a quiet virtual study buddy.
• Remote workers missing the gentle company of a shared desk.
• Writers, designers, and makers who create better beside other people.
• Neurodivergent minds and anyone who finds traditional productivity timers overwhelming.

Take a deep breath. Set your task. Join the Cozy Room—unhurried and calm.
